How I think about growth

Growth isn't about launching more campaigns.

It's about identifying the biggest commercial constraint preventing a business from scaling.

Sometimes the bottleneck is acquisition.
Sometimes it's positioning.
Sometimes it's activation, pricing, retention, or conversion.

My role is to identify that constraint, validate it with data, run experiments, and build systems that produce sustainable revenue growth.

Marketing activity isn't the goal.
Business growth is.

Start with customer insights
Growth starts with understanding customer behavior, motivations, and buying decisions.
Find the constraint
Every growth system has a limiting factor. Identifying it creates the highest leverage.
Experiment continuously
Small, measurable experiments reduce uncertainty and compound over time.
Scale what works
Successful growth comes from building repeatable systems — not one-time campaigns.
